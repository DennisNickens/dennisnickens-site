Doing It God's Way It Works

By Dennis S. Nickens, AKA "The Spiritual Romeo"  |  Published 2015  |  177 pages

Most people spend more time planning a wedding than preparing for a marriage. They chase chemistry, ignore compatibility, and wonder why the fire fizzles. Dennis S. Nickens, known as "The Spiritual Romeo," went straight to the Source. In Doing It God's Way It Works, he unpacks the original design for love, partnership, and covenant. Why God created man first and what he needed to learn before meeting her. The sacred reason Eve was formed from Adam's rib and why it changes how you see your spouse. The difference between a marriage contract and a blood covenant. This isn't another relationship book filled with feel good fluff. This is a spiritual roadmap for singles ready to stop playing games and couples ready to build on rock instead of sand.

Inside you will find the 10 questions every couple must answer before the altar, including the ones nobody wants to ask. How to transition from me to we without losing your God given purpose. Parental guidance on raising relationship ready children. Healing for those recovering from past hurts. Whether you are single, engaged, or guiding the next generation, this book shows you how to stop guessing at love and start building it God's way. Your future marriage deserves a foundation that lasts. Get your copy now and discover why doing it God's way is the only way that truly works.
