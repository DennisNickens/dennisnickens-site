Linked Pair Setup

Here's How Linked Pair Works

Six steps. The most important thing to know: you go first. Your partner does not need to be ready right now.

1

You go first.

You will complete your assessment now. Takes 15 to 30 minutes depending on the tier you pick next.

2

You get a Pair Code.

When you finish, you get a unique code (like SR-7K3M-92) and a shareable link to give your partner.

3

Share it with your partner.

Text it, email it, send it however you usually share things. Your partner uses the link or types the code to start their own assessment.

4

They take their assessment.

Same process you just went through. Their answers stay private until both of you are done.

5

Both Blueprints generate.

When your partner finishes, both of you get your individual Blueprint emailed within 5 minutes.

6

The Comparison Blueprint arrives.

A third Blueprint, built from both of yours, lands in both your inboxes. This is the one that shows how you fit together. Where you match. Where you will trip each other up. How to communicate so it lands.

Your partner does not need to be ready right now. Take your assessment first. Share the code when you are ready.
