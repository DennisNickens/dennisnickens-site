Three 10 pins means your ball is hitting flat, friend. Your Phaze II at 2000 grit is burning up early on that house shot. Two moves: slide your feet 2 boards left and keep your eyes on the same target, or add a little polish so the ball saves energy for the pocket. Try the feet first, it is free.
