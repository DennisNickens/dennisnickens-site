About the Book

Healing, Wholeness, and Identity Before "I Do"

In a culture that glorifies relationships and pressures people to "find someone" fast, Singlehood: It's OK to Be Selfish is a bold and necessary declaration: before you give yourself to someone else, it's okay, and essential, to focus on yourself.

Dennis Nickens delivers a transparent, faith-rooted, and empowering guide for anyone navigating life as a single person. Whether you're healing from heartbreak, growing through a season of self-discovery, or intentionally waiting for a purpose-aligned partner, this book challenges the idea that being single is something to escape.

Instead, Dennis reframes singlehood as a sacred season, one filled with opportunity, clarity, and preparation. He invites readers to embrace this time to rediscover their identity, restore their emotional health, and reset their standards, not out of selfishness, but out of self-awareness and self-worth.

Inside the book, you'll learn how to:

  • Break free from cycles of codependency, fear, or low self-esteem
  • Develop spiritual maturity and emotional intelligence as an individual
  • Set and enforce healthy relationship boundaries
  • Understand the difference between loneliness and divine solitude
  • Love yourself fully so you can love others well later

This isn't just a book, it's a personal revolution. Singlehood: It's OK to Be Selfish gives you permission to focus inward so when the time comes to love outward, you're doing it from a place of strength and purpose.

You're not broken. You're becoming. And this season is for you.
