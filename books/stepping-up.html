About the Book

Lead Boldly. Serve Authentically. Influence with Purpose.

Leadership isn't about titles, it's about taking responsibility, making hard decisions, and showing up with integrity when it matters most. In Stepping Up, Dennis Nickens offers a timely and transformative guide for individuals ready to lead with conviction in their homes, workplaces, ministries, and communities.

This book is for emerging and established leaders who understand that true leadership is about servanthood, character, and consistency, not control. With a balance of real-life insight, biblical wisdom, and practical tools, Dennis equips readers to face modern leadership challenges head-on while remaining grounded in purpose and guided by faith.

In this book, you will discover:

  • The difference between position and purpose
  • How to lead when you feel unqualified or unseen
  • The role of emotional intelligence in effective leadership
  • Tools for leading through conflict, criticism, and change
  • Biblical models of leadership and what they teach us today
  • How to build a legacy of influence and impact

Whether you're leading a business, a ministry, a team, or your family, Stepping Up is a call to rise above fear, comparison, and complacency. It's a roadmap for becoming the leader God designed you to be: bold, humble, and unshakable.

You don't need permission to lead, you just need the courage to step up.
